Frequently Asked Questions

Who created Spider-Man?

Spider-Man was created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko in 1962.

How many Spider-People exist in the Spider-Verse?

Hundreds — including Peter Parker, Miles Morales, Gwen Stacy, Spider‑Man Noir, and many more.

What makes Spider-Man unique?

His relatability, humor, and the idea that “anyone can wear the mask.”

Why does Spider-Man always struggle financially?

Because Peter Parker is written to be grounded and human — a hero with real‑world problems.
